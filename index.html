What we build

Every project is measured, drawn and priced before a single wall is touched.

Interior designing

Full residential and commercial layouts — space planning, material selection and finish schedules built around how you actually live, not a showroom catalogue.

Modular kitchen

Compact or open-plan kitchens fitted around your storage and cooking habits, with hardware chosen for daily use rather than launch-day shine.

How a project runs

Four stages, start to handover.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We visit the site, understand the brief and budget, and take measurements.

  2. 2

    Design & estimate

    Layouts, material boards and a itemised quote — no surprise line items later.

  3. 3

    Execution

    Civil, carpentry, electrical and false ceiling work run to the agreed schedule.

  4. 4

    Handover

    Final styling, a walkthrough, and a snag list cleared before we hand over the keys.
